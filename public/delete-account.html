Delete your MenoMind account

MenoMind (published by MenoMind, menomind.app) lets you delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1: In the app (immediate)

  1. Open MenoMind and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: By email

Email support@menomind.app from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my MenoMind account". We complete the deletion within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name, sign-in credentials)
  • Symptom logs, notes, trends and wellness plans
  • Chat conversations with MenoMind AI
  • Push notification tokens and app usage events

What is kept

  • Purchase and subscription records held by Apple, Google Play or Stripe for their own accounting, as required by law. Cancel any active subscription in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings; deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription.
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics.

See our Privacy Policy for details.
